# Create Customer 

Visitors to your website can open an account to manage their purchases and activities.

Customers generally create their own accounts in their shop. However, you can also create customer accounts directly from the administrator, which is useful for helping customers on the phone.

### Creating a customer from the admin panel

1. Go to **Customers >> Customers**.

2. Click the **Create Customer** button.

   <ImagePopup src="/images/customer/customer.png" alt="Customer" />

3. Fill in the necessary fields of the customer form, as shown in the below image.

   <ImagePopup src="/images/customer/create-customer.png" alt="Create Customer" />

4. Click **Save customer**. The new customer is registered and listed in the grid.

   <ImagePopup src="/images/customer/customer-grid.png" alt="Customer Grid" />

By the above steps, you can create a customer from the admin panel in Bagisto.
