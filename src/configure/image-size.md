# Image Size

The storefront shows product images in three sizes: small for thumbnails, medium for product cards, and large for the image gallery on the product page. The **Small Image**, **Medium Image** and **Large image** sections set the size of each, and the placeholder shown for products that have no image of their own.

1. Go to **Configure >> Catalog >> Products**.
2. In **Small Image**, **Medium Image** and **Large image**, enter the **Width** and **Height** in pixels.
3. Optionally, click **Add Image** under **Small Image Placeholder**, **Medium Image Placeholder** and **Large Image Placeholder**, and choose the image shown when a product has no image.
4. Click **Save Configuration**.

A placeholder can be a BMP, JPEG, JPG, PNG, WEBP or SVG file.
