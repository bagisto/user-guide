# Image Size

The storefront shows product images in three sizes. The **Small Image**, **Medium Image** and **Large image** sections set the size of each, and the placeholder shown for products that have no image of their own.

1. Go to **Configure >> Catalog >> Products**.
2. In **Small Image**, enter the **Width** in pixels.
3. Enter the **Height** in pixels.
4. Optionally, click **Add Image** under **Small Image Placeholder** and choose the image shown when a product has no image.
5. Repeat these steps in **Medium Image** and **Large image**.
6. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/products-image-size.png" alt="Small Image, Medium Image and Large image sections of the Products screen" />

| Size | Where it's used | Size when left empty |
|---|---|---|
| **Small Image** | The thumbnails in the product page gallery, and product cards on small screens | 100 × 100 pixels |
| **Medium Image** | Product cards in category pages, search results and carousels | 350 × 360 pixels |
| **Large image** | The main image in the product page gallery | 560 × 610 pixels |

Product images are cropped to fill the size you set. A placeholder can be a BMP, JPEG, JPG, PNG, WEBP or SVG file; without one, Bagisto's own placeholder image is shown. These settings apply to the whole store.
