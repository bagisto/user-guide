# Storefront

The **Storefront** section decides how products are listed on category and search pages when a shopper first opens them.

1. Go to **Configure >> Catalog >> Products**.
2. In **Storefront**, choose the **Default List Mode**.
3. Enter the **Products Per Page**.
4. Choose the **Sort By** order.
5. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/products-storefront.png" alt="Storefront section of the Products screen" />

All three settings are set per channel.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Default List Mode** | Whether products are shown as a **Grid** of cards or a **List** of rows. Shoppers can switch between the two on the page; this is the view they see first. |
| **Products Per Page** | The page sizes shoppers can choose from, separated by commas, such as `12,24,36`. The first value is used until a shopper chooses another. |
| **Sort By** | The order products are listed in until a shopper chooses another: **From A-Z**, **From Z-A**, **Newest First**, **Oldest First**, **Cheapest First** or **Expensive First**. |

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/products-storefront-listing.png" alt="Category page on the storefront with the sort order, page size and the list and grid buttons" />

## Differences in Bagisto 2.4

In Bagisto 2.4, this section also holds **Allow customers to directly buy products**, which is in [Product View Page](./product-view-page.md) in Bagisto 2.5. The search settings of the Bagisto 2.4 **Search** section are under [Search Engines](./search-engines.md) in Bagisto 2.5.
