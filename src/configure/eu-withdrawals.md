# EU Withdrawals

EU withdrawals let customers withdraw from a purchase online, as the EU requires for consumers under Directive (EU) 2023/2673 (Article 11a of the Consumer Rights Directive). The feature is turned on per channel. How customers withdraw and how you handle withdrawals is described in [EU Withdrawal](../orders/eu-withdrawal.md).

## Turn on EU withdrawals for a channel

1. Go to **Configure >> Sales >> EU Withdrawals**.
2. If your store has more than one channel, choose the channel at the top of the screen.
3. In **General**, switch **Enable** on.
4. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/eu-withdrawals-settings.png" alt="General section of the EU Withdrawals screen with Enable switched on" />

It's off in a new store. Turn it on only for channels that sell to consumers in the EU.

## What changes on the storefront

- Customers see a **Withdraw from Contract** button on their order pages, or **View your withdrawal** once they have withdrawn.
- Guests see a **Withdraw from a Contract** link in the profile menu of the storefront header on desktop screens. It opens a form where they look up their order. When the order number and email address match a guest order placed on the same channel, they receive an email with a link to withdraw, valid for 24 hours.
- While **Enable** is off, the button and link are hidden, and the withdrawal forms of the channel, including the guest lookup form, show a "not found" page. Receipts of withdrawals already submitted still open, and the withdrawals stay in **Sales >> EU Withdrawals**.
