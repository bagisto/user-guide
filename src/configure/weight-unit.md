# Weight Unit

The weight unit is the unit every product weight is entered in and every shipping carrier reads. Set it once, before you add products, because changing it later does not convert the weights already saved.

## Set the weight unit

1. Go to **Configure >> General >> General**.
2. In the **Unit Options** section, choose **lbs** or **kgs** from the **Weight Unit** dropdown.
3. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/weight.png" alt="Weight Unit setting" />

The setting is per channel, so switch channel at the top of the page if your channels ship in different units.
