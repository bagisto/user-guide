# Guest Checkout

Guest checkout allows customers to buy products without creating an account, streamlining the purchase process for convenience and faster transactions.

To manage Guest Checkout, go to the Admin Panel and click **Configure >> Sales >> Checkout**.  
In the **Shopping Cart** section, switch **Allow guest checkout** on or off, then click **Save Configuration**.

A product can still be kept out of guest orders on its own: the **Guest Checkout** switch on the product's edit page overrides this setting for that product.
