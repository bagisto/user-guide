# Guest Checkout

Guest checkout lets shoppers place an order without creating an account, which shortens the purchase for first-time buyers.

## Allow or block guest checkout

1. Go to **Configure >> Sales >> Checkout**.
2. In the **Shopping Cart** section, switch **Allow guest checkout** on or off.
3. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/guest-checkout.png" alt="Allow guest checkout switch" />

## Keep one product out of guest orders

A product can be excluded on its own, whatever the store-wide setting says:

1. Go to **Catalog >> Products** and open the product.
2. Switch **Guest Checkout** off in the product's settings.
3. Click **Save Product**.

A cart that contains such a product asks the shopper to sign in before checkout.
