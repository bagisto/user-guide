# Shipping

Shipping Origin refers to the location from which goods or products are dispatched. It is the starting point of the shipping process before items are transported to their destination.

To configure, go to the Admin Panel and navigate to **Configure >> Sales >> Shipping Settings**. The **Origin** section takes the country, state, city, address, postcode, store name, VAT number and contact of the place you ship from; it is used for tax calculation when taxes are based on the shipping origin, and printed on invoices.
