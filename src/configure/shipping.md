# Shipping Settings

The shipping origin is the address your orders are shipped from. It is used to calculate tax when **Calculation Based On** is set to **Shipping Origin** under [Taxes](./taxes.md), and your store details from this screen are printed on invoice PDFs.

## Set the shipping origin

1. Go to **Configure >> Sales >> Shipping Settings**.
2. In **Origin**, enter the **Country**, **State**, **City**, **Street Address** and **Zip** of the place you ship from. All five are required.
3. Optionally, enter the **Store Name**, **Vat Number**, **Contact Number** and **Bank Details** to print on invoices.
4. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/shipping-origin.png" alt="Origin section filled in with a New York address" />

The origin is set per channel, and the address, store name and bank details are also set per language. The shipping methods shoppers choose from are set under [Shipping Methods](../shipping-method/shipping-methods.md).
