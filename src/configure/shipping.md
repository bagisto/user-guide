# Shipping Settings

The shipping origin is the place your orders are dispatched from. It is used for tax calculation when taxes are based on the shipping origin, and it is printed on invoices.

1. Go to **Configure >> Sales >> Shipping Settings**.
2. In the **Origin** section, enter the **Country**, **State**, **City**, **Street Address**, **Zip** and **Store Name** of the place you ship from.
3. Optionally add the **Vat Number**, **Contact Number** and **Bank Details** to print on invoices.
4. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/shipping.png" alt="Shipping origin settings" />

The origin is per channel and per locale, so switch channel at the top of the page for a store that ships from somewhere else, and switch locale to translate the address and store name. The shipping methods themselves are configured under **Configure >> Sales >> Shipping Methods**; see [Shipping Methods](../shipping-method/shipping-methods.md).
