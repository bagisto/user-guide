# Product View Page

The **Product view page configuration** section decides how many related and up-sell products the product page shows, and whether shoppers can buy a product straight from its page. These settings apply to the whole store.

1. Go to **Configure >> Catalog >> Products**.
2. In **Product view page configuration**, enter the **Allowed number of Related Products**.
3. Enter the **Allowed number of Up-Sell Products**.
4. Switch **Allow customers to directly buy products** on or off.
5. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/products-product-view-page.png" alt="Product view page configuration section of the Products screen" />

**Allowed number of Related Products:** How many of the related products chosen on a product are shown on its page. Leave it empty to show all of them, or enter `0` to show none.

**Allowed number of Up-Sell Products:** How many of the up-sell products chosen on a product are shown on its page. Leave it empty to show all of them, or enter `0` to show none.

The related and up-sell products themselves are chosen on each product; see [Products](../product-types/products.md#related-up-sell-and-cross-sell-products).

**Allow customers to directly buy products:** Shows a **Buy Now** button beside **Add To Cart** on the product page. Shoppers who click it go straight to checkout with that product. The button isn't shown while **Cart Page** is switched off under [Checkout](./checkout.md).

## Differences in Bagisto 2.4

In Bagisto 2.4, **Allow customers to directly buy products** is in the [Storefront](./frontend.md) section.
