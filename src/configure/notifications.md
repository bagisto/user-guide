# Notifications

The **Notifications** screen decides which emails your store sends to customers, to admins and to inventory sources.

1. Go to **Configure >> Email >> Notifications**.
2. Switch each email on or off.
3. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/email-notifications.png" alt="Notifications screen with a switch for each email" />

| Switch | Sent to |
|---|---|
| **Send a confirmation e-mail after customer registration** | The customer, after they create an account. |
| **Send a confirmation e-mail to admin after customer registration** | The admin, after a customer creates an account. |
| **Send the customer account credentials after registration** | The customer, with their sign-in details, when you create their account in the admin panel. |
| **Send a confirmation e-mail to the customer after placing a new order** | The customer, after they place an order. |
| **Send a confirmation e-mail to the admin after placing a new order** | The admin, after an order is placed. |
| **Send a notification e-mail to the customer after creating a new invoice** | The customer, when an invoice is created. |
| **Send a notification e-mail to the admin after creating a new invoice** | The admin, when an invoice is created. |
| **Send a notification e-mail to the customer after creating a refund** | The customer, when a refund is created. |
| **Send a notification e-mail to the admin after creating a new refund** | The admin, when a refund is created. |
| **Send a notification e-mail to the customer after creating a shipment** | The customer, when a shipment is created. |
| **Send a notification e-mail to the admin after creating a new shipment** | The admin, when a shipment is created. |
| **Send a notification e-mail to the inventory source after creating a shipment** | The contact of the inventory source the shipment is sent from. |
| **Send a notification to customer after canceling an order** | The customer, when an order is canceled. |
| **Send a notification e-mail to admin after canceling an order** | The admin, when an order is canceled. |

Emails are sent from the addresses set under [Email Settings](./email-settings.md), and admin emails go to the **Admin Email Address** set there.
