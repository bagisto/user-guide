# Social Share

Social share adds share buttons to product pages, so shoppers can share a product on Facebook, X (Twitter), Pinterest, WhatsApp or LinkedIn, or by email.

1. Go to **Configure >> Catalog >> Products**.
2. In **Social Share**, switch **Enable Social Share?** on.
3. Switch on each share button you want to offer, described below.
4. Enter the **Share Message**.
5. Click **Save Configuration**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/configure/products-social-share.png" alt="Social Share section of the Products screen with every share button switched on" />

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable Social Share?** | Shows sharing on product pages. While it's off, product pages show no share buttons. |
| **Enable Share in Facebook?**, **Enable Share on X (Twitter)?** and **Enable Share in Pinterest?** | Each adds that network's share button. |
| **Enable Share on WhatsApp?** | Adds a WhatsApp button, which opens the WhatsApp app with the product's link. |
| **Enable Share in Linkedin?** and **Enable Share in Email?** | Each adds a LinkedIn or an email share button. |
| **Share Message** | The text shared with the product's link. |

On small screens, product pages show a single **Share** button instead, which opens the device's own sharing options. These settings apply to the whole store.
