# Virtual Product

A virtual product is something you sell that isn't shipped, such as a membership, a
service, a gift card or a monthly pass. It works like a [simple product](./simple-product.md),
without shipping: it has no weight or dimensions, and shoppers don't enter a shipping
address or choose a shipping method when their cart holds only products that aren't
shipped.

This page covers what's specific to virtual products. For the sections every product
shares, see [Products](./products.md).

## Create a virtual product

Create the product as described in [Create a product](./products.md#create-a-product),
choosing **Virtual** as the **Type**.

## How it differs from a simple product

- **No Shipping section:** there's no weight or size to enter.
- **No RMA section:** virtual products can't be returned through RMA.
- **Stock still counts:** the **Inventories** section is there, so you can limit how many
  you sell, such as 100 event passes. The back-order setting doesn't apply: once the stock
  runs out, the product can't be bought until you add more.
- **Customizable options:** like a simple product, a virtual product can have a
  **Customizable Item** section, such as a name to print on a gift card. See
  [Customizable options](./simple-product.md#customizable-options).

## What shoppers see

A virtual product page looks like a simple product's: shoppers choose a quantity and add it
to the cart.
