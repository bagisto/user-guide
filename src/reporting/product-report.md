# Products Reporting

Bagisto provides detailed insights into product performance through various reports. These reports help store owners track product sales, customer engagement, and search trends. Below is a breakdown of the available product-related reports.

### A) Sold Products Quantity

This report displays the total number of products sold within the selected date range.

**Key Metrics:**

- **Sold Products Quantity:** The total quantity of products sold during the specified period.
- **Percentage Change:** A comparison with the previous period to track growth or decline.
- **View Details:** Click to see detailed sales data.

### B) Quantities Sold Over Time

Provides a timeline-based view of product sales to help identify sales trends.

**How to Use:**

- Compare sales performance across different date ranges.
- Identify peak sales periods.
- Adjust inventory and marketing strategies based on demand.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/sold-products.png" alt="Sold Products" />

### C) Products Added To Wishlist

Shows how many products have been added to customers' wishlists over the selected period.

**Key Metrics:**

- **Products Added To Wishlist:** The number of times products were added to wishlists.
- **Percentage Change:** A comparison with the previous period.
- **View Details:** Click to see a list of the most wished-for products.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/products-wishlist.png" alt="Products Wishlist" />

### D) Top Selling Products By Revenue

Lists the products that have generated the most revenue within the selected period.

**Key Metrics:**

- **Product Name:** The name of the top-selling product.
- **Total Revenue:** The total revenue generated by the product.
- **View Details:** Click to see a detailed revenue breakdown.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/revenue-products.png" alt="Top Selling Products By Revenue" />

### E) Top Selling Products By Quantity

Displays products that have been sold the most in terms of quantity.

**Key Metrics:**

- **Product Name:** The most frequently purchased product.
- **Total Units Sold:** The total number of units sold.
- **View Details:** Click to see a breakdown of sales by product.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/products-quantity.png" alt="Top Selling Products By Quantity" />

### F) Products With Most Reviews

Shows which products have received the highest number of customer reviews.

**Key Metrics:**

- **Product Name:** The product with the most reviews.
- **Total Reviews:** The number of reviews received.
- **View Details:** Click to view customer feedback and ratings.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/product-review.png" alt="Products With Most Reviews" />

### G) Last Search Terms

Tracks the most recent search terms used by customers on the store.

**Key Metrics:**

- **Search Term:** The keyword or phrase searched by users.
- **Total Searches:** The number of times the term has been searched.
- **View Details:** Click to analyze customer search behavior.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/last-search-term.png" alt="Last Search Terms" />

### H) Top Search Terms

Displays the most frequently searched terms in the store within the selected date range.

**Key Metrics:**

- **Search Term:** The most commonly searched keyword or phrase.
- **Total Searches:** The number of times the term has been used.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/top-search-term.png" alt="Top Search Terms" />
