# Products Reporting

**Reporting >> Products** shows how your products perform: how many units sell, which products earn the most, what customers add to their wishlists and review, and what they search for.

## Choose the channel and dates

1. Go to **Reporting >> Products**.
2. If your store has more than one channel, choose a channel, or leave **All Channels**.
3. Click the date range at the top right.
4. Choose a preset, or pick a start and an end date on the calendar.

Every card reloads for the channel and dates you chose. The report opens on the last 30 days. The presets are listed in [Choose the channel and dates](sales-report.md#choose-the-channel-and-dates), and how figures are compared with the period before in [Reading the cards](sales-report.md#reading-the-cards).

## Sold Products Quantity

How many units sold on orders placed in the range, with the percentage change and a **Quantities Sold Over Time** chart. For units per product, see **Top Selling Products By Quantity**. The chart and its details table show sales activity over time and may not add up to the card's total.

## Products Added To Wishlist

How many times products were added to wishlists in the range, with the percentage change and a **Products Added Over Time** chart. Products later removed from a wishlist, or moved to the cart, no longer count.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/products-wishlist.png" alt="Products Added To Wishlist card with its chart" />

## Top Selling Products By Revenue

The five products that earned the most on orders placed in the range: the price times the quantity invoiced, minus refunds, without tax and before discounts. A product sold at more than one price is listed once for each price.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/revenue-products.png" alt="Top Selling Products By Revenue card" />

## Top Selling Products By Quantity

The five products with the most units invoiced, minus refunded units, on orders placed in the range. A product sold at more than one price is listed once for each price.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/products-quantity.png" alt="Top Selling Products By Quantity card" />

## Products With Most Reviews

The five products with the most approved reviews written in the range, including reviews by guests.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/product-review.png" alt="Products With Most Reviews card" />

## Last Search Terms

The terms shoppers searched for most recently in the range, newest first:

- **Search Term**: what the shopper typed in the storefront search.
- **Results**: how many products the last search found.
- **Uses**: how many times the term has been searched on that channel and in that language.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/last-search-term.png" alt="Last Search Terms card with results and uses" />

## Top Search Terms

The terms searched for most often, with the same **Search Term**, **Results** and **Uses** columns. This card counts every search ever made, so it doesn't change with the dates, only with the channel, and it has no **View Details**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/reporting/top-search-term.png" alt="Top Search Terms card" />

## View details and export

Every card except **Top Search Terms** has **View Details**, which opens the figures as a table.

1. Click **View Details** on a card.
2. If your store has more than one channel, choose the channel.
3. For **Sold Products Quantity** or **Products Added To Wishlist**, choose **Day**, **Month** or **Year**. For totals that cover every date you chose, use **Day**.
4. Choose the dates.

The details page opens on **All Channels** and the last 30 days. Click a column heading to sort the table on screen. **Back** returns to the report.

| Card | Table columns |
|---|---|
| **Sold Products Quantity** | **Interval**, **Quantities** |
| **Products Added To Wishlist** | **Interval**, **Total** |
| **Top Selling Products By Revenue** | **Id**, **Name**, **Price**, **Revenue** |
| **Top Selling Products By Quantity** | **Id**, **Name**, **Quantities** |
| **Products With Most Reviews** | **Id**, **Name**, **Reviews** |
| **Last Search Terms** | **Id**, **Search Term**, **Results**, **Uses**, **Channel** (the channel's ID), **Locale** |

### Export the table

1. Click **Export**.
2. Choose **Export CSV** or **Export XLS**.

The file covers the channel, period and dates chosen on the details page. The exported file keeps the original order, not the order you sorted on screen.

## Permissions

A role needs **Products** under **Reporting** to open this report, its details and its export.
