# Campaigns

E-commerce marketing campaigns are designed to drive traffic and increase sales. They can help you reach many potential customers, keep existing customers engaged, and build brand loyalty.

### Creating a campaign

1. Go to **Marketing >> Communications >> Campaigns**.

2. Click **Create Campaign**, as shown in the below image.

   <ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/campaign.png" alt="Campaign" />

3. Fill in the campaign fields, described below.

4. Click **Save Campaign**.

### Campaign fields

**1. Name** – Add the name of the campaign.  

**2. Subject** – Add the subject according to your requirements.  

**3. Event** – Select the event for which you want to apply the campaign. Only existing events are listed; to add a new one, create it first under **Marketing >> Communications >> Events**.  

**4. Email Template** – Select the Email template you want to use for sending campaign emails.  

**5. Channel** – Select the channel.  

**6. Customer Group** – Select the customer group to which you want to apply the campaign.  

By default, there are 3 customer groups:

- Guest
- General
- Wholesale

For the **General** and **Wholesale** customer groups, campaign emails are sent to the customers of that group who have subscribed to the newsletter. Whether the customer is logged in makes no difference. The emails are sent by the store's scheduler, which processes campaigns once a day.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/campaign-configration.png" alt="Campaign Fields" />

For the **Guest** customer group, emails are sent to the newsletter subscribers who do not have a customer account.

Customers who have registered an account do not receive the campaigns of the **Guest** group.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/guest-campaign.png" alt="Guest Customer Group Campaign" />

**7. Status** – Set the status to Active or Inactive.  

### Campaign created

After saving, the new **Campaign** is listed, as shown below.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/campaign-output.png" alt="Campaign Output" />

By this, you can create a **Campaign** in Bagisto.
