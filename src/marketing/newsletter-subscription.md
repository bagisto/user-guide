# Newsletter Subscriptions

Newsletter subscribers are the people who agreed to receive your marketing emails. [Campaigns](campaigns.md) go to people who subscribed to your newsletter, so this list is where your email marketing starts.

## How shoppers subscribe

- **In the storefront footer**, by entering their email and clicking **Subscribe**. The form is shown while **Allow Newsletter Subscription** is on. See [Newsletter subscription](../configure/settings.md#newsletter-subscription).
- **When they create an account**, by ticking **Subscribe to newsletter**. The checkbox is shown while **Allow NewsLetter** is on. See [Create new account options](../configure/settings.md#create-new-account-options).
- **On their account profile**, with the **Subscribe to newsletter** option.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/newsletter-footer.png" alt="The newsletter sign-up form in the storefront footer with an email field and the Subscribe button" />

Shoppers who subscribe in the footer get a confirmation email with a link to unsubscribe. Customers can also unsubscribe at any time by clearing **Subscribe to newsletter** on their profile.

## View subscribers

Go to **Marketing >> Communications >> Newsletter Subscriptions**. The list shows each subscriber's **ID**, whether they're **Subscribed**, and their **Email**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/newsletter-subscriptions.png" alt="The Newsletter Subscriptions list with two subscribers" />

There's no button to add subscribers yourself, because people subscribe from the storefront.

## Unsubscribe someone

1. Go to **Marketing >> Communications >> Newsletter Subscriptions**.
2. Click the edit icon of the subscriber. The **Edit Newsletter Subscriber** dialog opens.
3. Set **Subscribed** to **False**.
4. Click **Save Subscriber**.

<ImagePopup src="/images/marketing/newsletter-subscriber-edit.png" alt="The Edit Newsletter Subscriber dialog with the subscriber's email and the Subscribed setting" />

When the subscriber has a customer account, the newsletter option on their account changes too.

To remove someone from the list completely, click the delete icon of the subscriber. Deleting also clears **Subscribe to newsletter** on their customer account. For someone without a customer account who asked to unsubscribe, delete their entry, so that [Guest campaigns](campaigns.md#when-campaigns-are-sent) can't reach them.
